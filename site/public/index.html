loopup v0.0.1

Give an AI a goal. Watch it prove the work.

You state what you want. LoopUp figures out what "done" really means, races several attempts, keeps the one that survives real tests, remembers what it learned, and stops for your approval before anything irreversible. You get an answer you can actually trust — and a receipt anyone can re-check.

Pick something to try

Several AIs try it, a critic attacks each, only the tested answer survives.

This isn't a canned demo. Every run hits five separate live services in real time and takes about 10 seconds.
